# Code of conduct

English | [中文](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh.md)

## Our pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects and promotes the dignity, rights, and contributions of all individuals, regardless of characteristics including race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic position, level of education, or other status. The same privileges of participation are extended to everyone who participates in good faith and in accordance with this Code of Conduct.

## Encouraged behaviors

While acknowledging differences in social norms, we all strive to meet our community's expectations for positive behavior. We also understand that our words and actions may be interpreted differently than we intend based on culture, background, or native language.

With these considerations in mind, we agree to behave mindfully toward each other and act in ways that center our shared values, including:

1. Respecting the purpose of our community, our activities, and our ways of gathering.
2. Engaging kindly and honestly with others.
3. Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
4. Taking responsibility for our actions and contributions.
5. Gracefully giving and accepting constructive feedback.
6. Committing to repairing harm when it occurs.
7. Behaving in other ways that promote and sustain the well-being of our community.

## Restricted behaviors

We agree to restrict the following behaviors in our community. Instances, threats, and promotion of these behaviors are violations of this Code of Conduct.

1. Harassment. Violating explicitly expressed boundaries or engaging in unnecessary personal attention after any clear request to stop.
2. Character attacks. Making insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments directed at a community member or group of people.
3. Stereotyping or discrimination. Characterizing anyone's personality or behavior on the basis of immutable identities or traits.
4. Sexualization. Behaving in a way that would generally be considered inappropriately intimate in the context or purpose of the community.
5. Violating confidentiality. Sharing or acting on someone's personal or private information without their permission.
6. Endangerment. Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm toward any person or group.
7. Behaving in other ways that threaten the well-being of our community.

### Other restrictions

1. Misleading identity. Impersonating someone else for any reason, or pretending to be someone else to evade enforcement actions.
2. Failing to credit sources. Not properly crediting the sources of content you contribute.
3. Promotional materials. Sharing marketing or other commercial content in a way that is outside the norms of the community.
4. Irresponsible communication. Failing to responsibly present content which includes, links to, or describes any other restricted behaviors.

## Reporting

Not every disagreement or technical criticism is a Code of Conduct violation. The way people treat one another during a disagreement can be.

Ruokee, the project maintainer, receives and handles conduct reports. Report suspected violations privately to Ruokee at [ruokee@foxmail.com](mailto:ruokee@foxmail.com) rather than in a public Issue or pull request. Include what happened, where and when it happened, relevant links or records, and whether the conduct is ongoing. Include only personal information relevant to the report.

Reports are handled privately. Details are shared with involved parties only as needed to understand and address the incident. Ruokee reviews the information provided, may ask for clarification, and determines a response based on the circumstances and severity. No fixed response or resolution deadline is promised.

### Reports involving the maintainer

This project has no separate conduct committee or independent appeals body. A report concerning Ruokee would be received by the same person and cannot receive independent review within this project. No alternative recipient is designated.

## Responses

Ruokee may remove or edit violating content and restrict participation in project-managed spaces. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact, with the aim of stopping harm and enabling constructive participation where possible.

The following guidelines describe possible responses. Serious violations may justify skipping earlier steps.

1. Warning. Explain the violation privately in writing and ask the person to stop. Acknowledging responsibility, clarifying expectations, or apologizing privately may help repair harm.
2. Temporarily limited activities. Restrict participation in particular channels or interactions for a stated period when a warning is insufficient or the conduct is more serious.
3. Temporary suspension. Suspend participation in project-managed spaces for a stated period after a serious violation or repeated violations. Explain the conditions for returning.
4. Permanent ban. End participation in project-managed spaces when repeated violations persist despite other measures, or when the severity of a violation makes continued participation unsafe.

Where feasible, explain the reason for a restriction to the person affected without unnecessarily disclosing private report information.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Ruokee Agent Kit's repository, Issues, pull requests, and other discussion spaces explicitly managed by the project. It applies to maintainers, contributors, and other participants.

It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public or other spaces, such as through an official project account or as an appointed representative. Merely using the project's software or contributing to it does not make someone an official representative.
