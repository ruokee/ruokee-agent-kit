# Execution constraints

Read when entering Execute or Supervise, or when Clarify forms a Plan and resolves dependencies and permission. The execution restrictions below govern Execute and Supervise; reading them during Clarify neither starts execution nor removes its permitted interview method.

## Resolve scope

A named unit or subset limits the assignment. Otherwise start at the current or first unfinished dependency-ready unit and continue the remaining Plan. A session usually handles one unit at a time and may complete several serially. Quoted controls, examples, and negations do not alter scope or permission.

Distinguish the Plan's full scope from implementation readiness. Keep pending subtask clarification and blocked work visible in the parent Plan. Route required preparation to Clarify under the effective scope and permissions, not to an implementation Worker. Read the resulting child materials and update the parent's next step, dependencies, and links before implementing that work. Continue unrelated authorized ready units; neither preparation completion nor finishing those units completes the unresolved scope.

Implementation, repair, self-check, independent Verify, and necessary rechecks continue without routine intermediate confirmation. Neither `auto` nor `noask` is required. `confirm` requests item-by-item confirmation without removing required checks. Preserve any explicit human checkpoint.

During Execute and Supervise, do not call structured question tools such as `ask`, including through wrappers or other Agents. Read available facts and make authorized choices. Raise an indispensable user-only decision in ordinary text while continuing unaffected ready work. An explicit `noask` also prohibits structured questions; preserve that meaning during recovery.

## Keep permission separate

An execution request normally authorizes ordinary in-scope implementation, repair, project-local preparation, checks, temporary processes, and local candidate-preservation operations required by the project. Reuse valid grants rather than repeatedly requesting them. Authorized temporary-directory file checks remain within their check scope.

Execution does not automatically grant proposal approval, archival, integration, push, publication, local installation, expense, privileged operations, or Task closure. Follow project-specific requirements and narrower instructions. Verification is not user acceptance, and acceptance grants no unrelated action. Resolve foreseeable consequential requirement choices before implementation rather than postponing every approval until the end.

Missing permission, an unresolved requirement, or required external state blocks only dependent actions. Continue authorized ready work. Preserve the whole selected scope; do not relabel its completed subset as the full result. A material or design defect returns affected work to Clarify, while an implementation deviation is repaired in Execute.

## Use verified dependencies

Where project rules permit, preserve a verified predecessor with local task-owned commits and start dependent work from that frozen candidate before integration or final user review. Record each unit's base and candidate, and keep its change independently reviewable. Do not mix later work into a completed predecessor or merge merely to begin the next unit.

A branch cannot replace a genuinely required deployment, service, or published artifact. If a predecessor changes, refresh affected descendants, checks, and Verify evidence. Keep unintegrated candidates available for ordered delivery under its own permission.

A Worker stops at its assigned boundary; the execution owner continues the wider authorized Plan. Neither a Worker's stop nor a unit's pass requests another routine continue instruction.
